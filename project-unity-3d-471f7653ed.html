3D Chess

Overview

A 3D chess game with a board, selectable pieces, and turn-based play.

The board provides a three-dimensional view of a familiar turn-based strategy game. Play revolves around choosing a piece and deciding where it should move in relation to the opposing side. The overview presents the board and piece interaction, while the game-specific summary identifies any verified rules, camera controls, or move-history features supported by this version.

What it includes

  • Chessboard and pieces
  • Turn-based decisions
  • Board interaction
