Connect Four

Overview

Drop pieces into a vertical grid and try to connect four of your own. A move changes both the visible pattern and the available landing positions in that column.

The strategy comes from creating multiple possible lines while blocking the opposing side. Because pieces stack from the bottom, future opportunities depend on the structure already built on the board. The overview introduces the board game and shows its interface; the demo supplies this version’s playable interaction.

What it includes

  • Column-based piece placement
  • Four-in-a-row objective
  • Turn-based strategy
