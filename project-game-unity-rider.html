Neon Rider

Overview

A browser adaptation of the Unity Rider controller: accelerate along a neon track and rotate while airborne.

The player follows a route while controlling speed, direction, or the vehicle’s position. Turns and obstacles make it necessary to read the track ahead and react before contact. The overview presents the course and the driving interaction, with this version’s particular camera, restart, or airborne behavior identified in its summary.

What it includes

  • Route navigation
  • Vehicle or runner control
  • Obstacle avoidance
