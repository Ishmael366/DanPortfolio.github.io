Experience

A browser adaptation of the Unity Rider controller: accelerate along a neon track and rotate while airborne.

02

Description

A driving route has to be read before the vehicle reaches it. Steering and speed work together: a line that is safe at one pace can become difficult to follow when there is less time to react.

The project-specific description identifies the track, camera, checkpoints, or airborne movement present in this version. The central interaction is to keep the vehicle in a useful position as the route continues.

03

Detailed features

Route navigation

Movement and viewpoint work together to make the space readable. The player uses the controls to choose a route, judge distance, and position the character for the next interaction.

Vehicle or runner control

Driving connects direction and speed with the route ahead. Course geometry and checkpoints turn vehicle control into a sequence of spatial and timing decisions.

Obstacle avoidance

This part of the project contributes to the core interaction described above. Its role is presented through the project’s available interface, source, and visual preview.
