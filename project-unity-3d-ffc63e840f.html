Level Worlds

Overview

A countryside environment project focused on the arrangement of terrain, vegetation, and built elements. Its purpose is to present a coherent outdoor scene through level design.

The environment is evaluated through its composition rather than a separate game objective. Landforms, paths, and scene elements establish how the space is read and how its parts relate to one another. The published browser export currently opens a different sample scene, so it is not evidence of a verified countryside gameplay build.

What it includes

  • Outdoor scene layout
  • Level composition
  • Countryside environment source
