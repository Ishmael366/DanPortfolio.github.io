Terrible Tweeters

Overview

Launch a bird into destructible block towers across two physics-based levels.

The player chooses a launch and observes how the resulting movement interacts with the target structure. The scene turns direction, force, and collision into a visible sequence of cause and effect. The overview presents the launch setup and the objects in the level, while the demo supplies the available physics-based challenge.

What it includes

  • Launched character movement
  • Object collisions
  • Target structures
