01

Experience

Guide a snake through the play area while collecting items and avoiding collisions. As the snake grows, its own path becomes part of the obstacle to navigate.

02

Description

Each pickup extends the obstacle created by the snake itself. A turn must account for the direction of the head and the path the rest of the body will follow.

The challenge is to create a route that remains usable several moves later. Collection, growth, and collision make progress visible without needing a complex interface.

03

Detailed features

Directional movement

Movement and viewpoint work together to make the space readable. The player uses the controls to choose a route, judge distance, and position the character for the next interaction.

Item collection

Collectible objects connect exploration with measurable progress. Finding them gives the player a reason to examine the space and confirms that a route or objective has been completed.

Growth and collision avoidance

Collision links movement to the physical layout of a level. Contact with surfaces or objects changes what can move, where it can travel, or how an interaction resolves.
