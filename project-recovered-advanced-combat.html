Core systems

Character movement

Movement and viewpoint work together to make the space readable. The player uses the controls to choose a route, judge distance, and position the character for the next interaction.

Combat interactions

Close-range combat depends on distance and timing. Movement brings the character into range, while an attack creates a short opportunity to affect a nearby opponent.

Encounter presentation

An encounter groups threats and objectives into a readable sequence. Progress is communicated through the state of the participating characters and the result of their actions.

Play the browser version

Defeat six opponents. F attacks; Q dodges. Independent browser adaptation of the project concept, built with JavaScript and WebGL. This is not a packaged Unreal Engine build.
