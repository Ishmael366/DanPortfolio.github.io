Tetris

Overview

Arrange falling geometric pieces to complete horizontal lines. Each piece adds another placement decision as the stack grows and the remaining space changes.

The challenge comes from balancing immediate placement with the shapes and gaps left for later pieces. Completed lines clear space, while poorly placed blocks make subsequent decisions harder. This version’s demo presents its own controls and scoring behavior; the overview introduces the recognizable falling-block puzzle and its visual layout.

What it includes

  • Falling pieces
  • Block placement
  • Line clearing
