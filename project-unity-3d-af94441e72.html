3D Snake

Overview

Guide a snake through the play area while collecting items and avoiding collisions. As the snake grows, its own path becomes part of the obstacle to navigate.

The challenge increases as less open space remains available for turning. A useful route must account for where the head is moving and where the rest of the body will follow. The overview presents the board or three-dimensional arena used by this version, keeping the focus on the movement-and-collection loop.

What it includes

  • Directional movement
  • Item collection
  • Growth and collision avoidance
