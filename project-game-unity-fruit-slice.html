Fruit Ninja

Overview

Slice flying fruit into physical halves with a moving blade.

The player reacts to fruit moving through the play area and directs a slicing action across it. Motion and timing make the interaction feel immediate, while the resulting cut provides visible feedback. The overview shows the fruit and slicing presentation; the game-specific summary describes physical halves or other behaviors only where they are supported by that version.

What it includes

  • Moving fruit targets
  • Slicing interaction
  • Immediate visual feedback
