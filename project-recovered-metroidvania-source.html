Metroidvania

Overview

A side-view exploration and platforming game project. Movement through connected spaces forms the foundation of its world, with the character and level layout presented together.

The overview focuses on navigating the available game environment and understanding its routes. Its side-on view makes platforms, obstacles, and passages readable during movement. The project is described through its source and preview without inventing a completed campaign, additional areas, or progression systems that have not been verified.

What it includes

  • Side-view movement
  • Platform traversal
  • Environment exploration
