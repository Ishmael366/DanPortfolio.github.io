Dark Fantasy Combat

Overview

A combat project focused on character movement, attack timing, and enemy encounters.

Character positioning and the timing of attacks are central to this combat project. Encounters are presented through a controllable character in a game scene, bringing movement and offensive or defensive actions together. The overview focuses on those visible systems and the project’s setting; it does not add an unsupported story, campaign length, or multiplayer feature.

What it includes

  • Character movement
  • Combat interactions
  • Encounter presentation
