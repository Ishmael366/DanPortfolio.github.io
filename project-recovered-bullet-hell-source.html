Bullet Hell

Overview

A top-down action game centered on movement through projectile-filled encounters. The player must read the space around the character and react to threats while continuing the action.

The top-down viewpoint keeps nearby hazards and possible routes visible at the same time. Successful movement depends on finding openings rather than simply traveling in a straight line. The overview presents the arena and the projectile interaction, with the available demo showing the behavior of this particular version.

What it includes

  • Top-down movement
  • Projectile threats
  • Encounter navigation
