01

Experience

A top-down action game centered on movement through projectile-filled encounters. The player must read the space around the character and react to threats while continuing the action.

02

Description

The overhead view makes nearby threats and possible routes visible together. The player must find openings while continuing to respond to the action around the character.

Projectile patterns make the space change over time. Position and timing matter because a route that is open now may become unsafe during the next part of the encounter.

03

Detailed features

Top-down movement

Movement and viewpoint work together to make the space readable. The player uses the controls to choose a route, judge distance, and position the character for the next interaction.

Projectile threats

This part of the project contributes to the core interaction described above. Its role is presented through the project’s available interface, source, and visual preview.

Encounter navigation

An encounter groups threats and objectives into a readable sequence. Progress is communicated through the state of the participating characters and the result of their actions.
