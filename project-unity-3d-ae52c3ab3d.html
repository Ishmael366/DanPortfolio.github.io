Survival Horror

Overview

A first-person horror game combining exploration, tense environments, and survival mechanics.

The experience is built around moving through an unsettling environment and reading the space for a route forward. Camera perspective, limited visibility, and the arrangement of rooms or outdoor areas contribute to its atmosphere. The overview explains the exploration experience and shows the actual game setting, so the project can be understood even before a visitor opens the available demo.

What it includes

  • Environment exploration
  • Atmospheric scene presentation
  • Player and camera interaction
