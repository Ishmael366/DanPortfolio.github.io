Wind Meadow

Overview

A Unity game featuring rolling terrain and collision.

This scene places character movement within rolling outdoor terrain. Ground shape and collision affect how the player travels through the meadow, while the scene’s visual composition emphasizes the surrounding landscape. The overview presents the environment and the traversal interaction together, with the current browser prototype accessible through Demo.

What it includes

  • Rolling terrain
  • Character traversal
  • World collision
