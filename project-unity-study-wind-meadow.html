Experience

Move through rolling outdoor terrain and read the shape of the ground through the character’s movement and collisions.

02

Description

The scene is built around the relationship between a controllable character and uneven terrain. Slopes and changes in elevation make the ground itself part of the interaction rather than a flat decorative backdrop.

The project concentrates on traversal and world collision in a meadow setting. It is an environment-and-controller experience, not evidence of an additional combat campaign.

03

Detailed features

Rolling terrain

The environment provides the playable or visual setting. Landforms, landmarks, and surface detail give the space structure and make routes through it recognizable.

Character traversal

Movement and viewpoint work together to make the space readable. The player uses the controls to choose a route, judge distance, and position the character for the next interaction.

World collision

Collision links movement to the physical layout of a level. Contact with surfaces or objects changes what can move, where it can travel, or how an interaction resolves.
