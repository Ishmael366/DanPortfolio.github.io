Experience

Navigate a first-person shooting scene while managing ammunition and the timing of reloads.

02

Description

The first-person camera makes the visible space the basis for aiming. Ammunition limits how long the player can continue firing before a reload interrupts the interaction.

The reload interval adds a timing decision to the weapon loop. The available build focuses on movement, shooting, ammunition, and that reload state.

03

Detailed features

Character and camera control

Movement and viewpoint work together to make the space readable. The player uses the controls to choose a route, judge distance, and position the character for the next interaction.

Aiming and weapon use

The player chooses a direction or target and commits an attack. Visible hit feedback connects the input to its result, making accuracy and positioning part of the interaction.
