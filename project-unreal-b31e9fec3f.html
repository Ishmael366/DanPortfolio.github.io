Experience

A first-person target arena with aiming, shooting, and reactive targets.

02

Description

Target placement turns aiming into a readable spatial challenge. The player compares the target with the camera view and chooses where to direct the next shot.

The range keeps the focus on player control, aiming, and target feedback. The source and available presentation establish the project’s scope without an invented campaign or online mode.

03

Detailed features

First-person aiming

The player chooses a direction or target and commits an attack. Visible hit feedback connects the input to its result, making accuracy and positioning part of the interaction.

Target interaction

Targets provide a readable goal for interaction. Their position establishes the aiming challenge, while their response shows whether the action succeeded.

Arena layout

This part of the project contributes to the core interaction described above. Its role is presented through the project’s available interface, source, and visual preview.
