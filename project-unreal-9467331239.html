Rockfall Arena

Overview

An arena combining movement, physics objects, and interactive obstacles.

Movement takes place around physical objects that make the arena an interactive space. The relationship between the character, obstacles, and collisions is the main focus of the project. The overview shows that arena setup and its visual presentation without implying an unverified sequence of levels or multiplayer mode.

What it includes

  • Arena movement
  • Physical objects
  • Collision interaction
