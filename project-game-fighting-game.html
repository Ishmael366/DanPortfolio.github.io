02

Description

Two fighters share a compact arena. Their distance from one another determines whether an attack can connect, while the animation communicates when that action is taking place.

The health bars make the outcome of successful attacks visible across the round. The project concentrates on the two-player combat interaction and its sprite presentation.

03

Detailed features

Two-player combat

This part of the project contributes to the core interaction described above. Its role is presented through the project’s available interface, source, and visual preview.

Animated attacks

Close-range combat depends on distance and timing. Movement brings the character into range, while an attack creates a short opportunity to affect a nearby opponent.

Health feedback

A visible status value communicates the character’s condition. Changes in that value give actions consequences and make survival or recovery part of the play loop.
