Experience

Choose a destination in the world and let the character move toward it while responding to scene collisions.

02

Description

Click-to-move changes the relationship between input and movement: the player selects a place rather than holding a direction continuously. The scene must then make that selected destination readable.

World collision constrains the route through the shrine environment. The controller and the layout together determine how the character reaches the chosen position.

03

Detailed features

World exploration

Movement and viewpoint work together to make the space readable. The player uses the controls to choose a route, judge distance, and position the character for the next interaction.
