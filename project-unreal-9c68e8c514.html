01

Experience

An endless runner with health, shields, power-ups, and a risk-based score multiplier, supported by persistent settings and a detailed status HUD.

02

Description

The run is more forgiving and more systemic than a single-contact failure loop. Health and shields absorb different kinds of danger, including immediate damage and damage over time. Pickups can restore health, provide healing or shielding, change speed, or boost scoring.

The player accumulates points while surviving. Staying close to a wall increases the risk and the score multiplier. Menus accept a player name, expose adjustable movement settings, and show saved high scores after a run. The HUD makes health, shield state, double-jump availability, active effects, and scoring changes visible during play.

03

Detailed features

Health, shields and timed effects

The runner replaces instant death with depletable health and a shield. Different damage types and timed status effects create a stateful survival system, with pickups supplying recovery or temporary advantages.

Risk-based scoring and persistence

Survival earns points, while proximity to a wall increases the multiplier. High scores are saved and shown after a run. Movement settings are also saved through the settings menu.

HUD and level management

The interface displays animated health changes, shield status, double-jump cooldown, timed effect icons, and multiplier changes. Game Mode holds level arrays, and named test routes can be selected through a documented test mode.

04

Build summary

The project integrates native C++ with Blueprints and Unreal Motion Graphics. Gameplay state, level selection, configurable movement, saved settings and scores, menu flow, and the HUD are distinct parts of the documented implementation.
