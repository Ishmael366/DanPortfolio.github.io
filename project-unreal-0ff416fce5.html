First-Person Foundations

Overview

A first-person game project that puts the player’s viewpoint directly inside a three-dimensional scene. Movement and camera control provide the foundation for exploring and interacting with its levels.

The first-person perspective ties navigation closely to what is visible ahead of the character. The project brings scene layout and player control together so that spaces are experienced from ground level. The overview introduces that interaction and shows the project visually, with the available demo or source accessible through the corresponding action.

What it includes

  • First-person perspective
  • Player movement
  • Three-dimensional scene exploration
