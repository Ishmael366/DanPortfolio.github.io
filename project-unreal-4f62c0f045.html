First-Person Combat

Overview

A combat project focused on character movement, attack timing, and enemy encounters.

The player moves through a three-dimensional arena, lines up shots, and responds to the space around the character. Movement and weapon use form a single interaction loop: positioning affects what can be seen and targeted, while aiming determines where attacks are directed. The overview concentrates on the available shooting experience and its environment; any limitations of the published preview remain visible.

What it includes

  • Character and camera control
  • Aiming and weapon use
  • Arena navigation
