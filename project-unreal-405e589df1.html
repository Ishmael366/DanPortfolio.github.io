World Explorer

Overview

A 3D environment combining scene exploration, character movement, and interactive objects.

The project brings a controllable character into a game world designed around exploration. Movement connects different parts of the scene, while its layout and interactive elements give the player a reason to examine the surroundings. The overview introduces the setting and the central interaction without assigning an unverified campaign length or additional game modes.

What it includes

  • World exploration
  • Character control
  • Environment interaction
