Description

The player must divide attention between the direction of the next attack and the space needed to avoid an approaching obstacle or enemy. Projectiles make the result of aiming visible across the field.

The game-specific summary identifies the scoring, waves, effects, or movement behavior verified for this version. The common experience is a repeating cycle of positioning, attacking, and reacting to new danger.

03

Detailed features

Arcade movement

Movement and viewpoint work together to make the space readable. The player uses the controls to choose a route, judge distance, and position the character for the next interaction.

Projectile interaction

The player chooses a direction or target and commits an attack. Visible hit feedback connects the input to its result, making accuracy and positioning part of the interaction.

Threat avoidance

The player reads a moving or hidden threat and chooses a safer position. Avoidance makes timing and route selection central to completing the objective.
