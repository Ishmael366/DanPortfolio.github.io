Creek Environment

Overview

A natural environment with a creek landscape, foliage, terrain, and outdoor lighting.

This project concentrates on the construction and visual presentation of a three-dimensional environment. Terrain shapes establish the space, surface materials distinguish its ground and structures, and lighting gives those forms depth. The overview follows the scene itself, with attention to composition and the way its environmental elements work together. It is presented as an explorable or visual environment rather than a game with an invented scoring system.

What it includes

  • Terrain and scene layout
  • Environmental materials
  • Lighting and visual composition
