Pac-Man

Overview

Navigate a maze, collect pellets, and avoid the moving enemies sharing its corridors. The maze makes route choice and timing part of every movement decision.

The player weighs the opportunity to clear another part of the board against the position of nearby threats. Narrow paths and intersections make changes of direction important. The overview presents the maze and the central chase-and-collection loop, while the demo provides the behavior of this particular implementation.

What it includes

  • Maze navigation
  • Pellet collection
  • Moving enemy avoidance
