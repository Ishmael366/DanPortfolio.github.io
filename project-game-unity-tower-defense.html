Enemy routes

Other actors create a changing situation for the player to read. Their positions and behavior make movement and timing relevant to the next decision.

Wave-based encounters

An encounter groups threats and objectives into a readable sequence. Progress is communicated through the state of the participating characters and the result of their actions.
