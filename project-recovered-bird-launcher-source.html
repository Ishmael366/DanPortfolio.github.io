02

Description

The launch is a setup decision that plays out through movement and impact. Direction and force determine where the character travels and which part of the structure it reaches.

The resulting motion makes cause and effect visible. The game-specific summary identifies the destructible objects and level structure supported by this version.

03

Detailed features

Launched character movement

Movement and viewpoint work together to make the space readable. The player uses the controls to choose a route, judge distance, and position the character for the next interaction.

Object collisions

Collision links movement to the physical layout of a level. Contact with surfaces or objects changes what can move, where it can travel, or how an interaction resolves.

Target structures

Targets provide a readable goal for interaction. Their position establishes the aiming challenge, while their response shows whether the action succeeded.
