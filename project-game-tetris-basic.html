Experience

Arrange falling geometric pieces to complete horizontal lines. Each piece adds another placement decision as the stack grows and the remaining space changes.

02

Description

Each placement changes the contour of the stack. A useful move solves the immediate fit while avoiding holes or narrow spaces that will be difficult to fill later. Completing a horizontal line clears it and creates room to keep playing.

The experience combines quick spatial decisions with longer-term board management. The visible stack, the active piece, and the result of a cleared line make the current state understandable at a glance.
