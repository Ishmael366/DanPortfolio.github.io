Mad Doctor Shooter

Core systems

Character movement

Movement and viewpoint work together to make the space readable. The player uses the controls to choose a route, judge distance, and position the character for the next interaction.

Weapon interaction

The player chooses a direction or target and commits an attack. Visible hit feedback connects the input to its result, making accuracy and positioning part of the interaction.
