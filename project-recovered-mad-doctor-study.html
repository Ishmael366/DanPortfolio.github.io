Mad Doctor Shooter

Overview

A Unity action shooter centered on moving through a game scene and firing at threats. The project combines character control and weapon interaction in a compact playable format.

Its central loop is to move into a useful position, aim, and respond to the action on screen. The overview presents the game scene and shooting interaction, while the browser export can be opened separately from the card. The focus is the playable shooter system rather than an unsupported claim about additional missions or modes.

What it includes

  • Character movement
  • Weapon interaction
  • Shooter gameplay
