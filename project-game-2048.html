01

Experience

Slide numbered tiles across a grid and combine equal values into larger ones. Each move shifts the board, making the arrangement of open spaces part of the puzzle.

02

Description

A directional move shifts multiple tiles at once. Equal values can combine into a larger number, but the same move also changes the positions that will be available for the next merge.

The challenge is to preserve useful space while building larger tiles. A board that is easy to move through now may be more valuable than an immediate merge that leaves the remaining pieces awkwardly arranged.
