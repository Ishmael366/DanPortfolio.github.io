2048

Overview

Slide numbered tiles across a grid and combine equal values into larger ones. Each move shifts the board, making the arrangement of open spaces part of the puzzle.

The player plans merges while trying to preserve enough room for later moves. Useful combinations increase tile values, but a crowded board limits the directions that remain effective. The overview presents the number-merging loop and the visual state of the board before visitors launch the game.

What it includes

  • Sliding tile grid
  • Equal-value merges
  • Space management
